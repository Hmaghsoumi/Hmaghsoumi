# Hi, I'm Hossein Maghsoumi 👋

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the University of Central Florida, specializing in **Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Autonomous Driving**.

My research focuses on developing intelligent learning-based methods for autonomous systems, with applications in perception, decision-making, domain adaptation, connected vehicles, and autonomous racing.

## 🔬 Research Interests

- Machine Learning and Deep Learning
- Autonomous Driving
- Computer Vision for Intelligent Vehicles
- Domain Adaptation and Transfer Learning
- Autonomous Racing and Robotics
- Connected and Cooperative Vehicles

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### [Weather-Aware Domain Adaptation](https://github.com/Hmaghsoumi/Weather-Aware-Domain-Adaptation-for-Street-View-Weather-Recognition)

Deep learning and domain adaptation methods for robust street-view weather recognition under changing environmental conditions.

### [F1SIXTH](https://github.com/Hmaghsoumi/F1SIXTH)

A small-scale autonomous driving and racing platform for studying perception, planning, control, and real-world deployment challenges.

### [ConvoyNext](https://github.com/Hmaghsoumi/ConvoyNext)

A research platform for connected and cooperative autonomous vehicle platooning.

### [Trace-Driven ns-3 Workflow](https://github.com/Hmaghsoumi/trace-driven-ns3-workflow)

A workflow for converting real-world connected-vehicle field-test data into simulation-ready inputs for ns-3.
